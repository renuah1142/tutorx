Welcome to TutorX

Your roadmap to Tier 1 network support

A guided, self-paced course built to help you understand core networking, practise troubleshooting, and prepare to speak confidently in an interview.

How to get the most from TutorX

  1. Study one short lesson at a time.
  2. Explain key concepts in your own words.
  3. Use flashcards before checking answers.
  4. Take the section quiz and read every explanation.
  5. Practise scenarios as if you were handling a real support ticket.
  6. Revisit difficult topics until you can explain them without prompts.
Recommended target: 30 to 45 minutes a day, 5 days per week. Consistency and retrieval practice beat cramming.

About the content

TutorX teaches vendor-neutral fundamentals first. Real production commands, access permissions, customer privacy, provider SLAs, escalation rules, and change procedures vary by employer. Always follow the policies and authorization boundaries of the network you support.
